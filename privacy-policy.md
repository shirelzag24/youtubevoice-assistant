Privacy Policy
YouTube Voice Assistant (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: the extension listens only to run your voice commands on YouTube. It does not record or send your voice, and it never sends what you said, the songs you search for, or any link to the developer. It reports anonymous counts of which type of action was used, so the developer can improve the extension.

1. What the extension does
YouTube Voice Assistant lets you control YouTube with your voice, hands-free: pause and resume, rewind and fast-forward, go to a time in the video, skip ads, move to the next or previous song, change volume, speed, subtitles and full screen, and play a song or video by name. It works only on YouTube pages (youtube.com and its subdomains).

2. Voice and microphone
When you say the wake phrase (for example “Hey YouTube”) or press the microphone button in the player, the extension uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition. Chrome asks for your permission to use the microphone, and you can withdraw it at any time in the browser’s settings.
Speech recognition is performed by the browser’s speech service (in Chrome this is provided by Google) and is subject to Google’s privacy terms. The extension itself does not record, store or send your audio, and the developer never receives audio or transcripts.
The extension reads the recognized text only inside your browser, to decide which command to run. When the assistant asks a clarifying question, it may read it aloud with your browser’s text-to-speech voices. Some voices may be provided online by your browser or operating system.
3. Information kept on your device
Small settings stored in YouTube’s local storage in your browser: your preferred listening languages and whether the welcome tip was already shown.
When a voice search needs a page reload, the search phrase is kept briefly in your browser’s session storage and is deleted as soon as it is used (and in any case when the tab is closed). It stays on your device.
4. Voice search for songs and videos
When you ask to play a song or video, the extension enters the phrase into YouTube’s own search and opens a result. That search is handled by YouTube and Google like any search you type there, under their terms. The developer does not receive it.

5. Anonymous usage statistics
The extension sends a small number of anonymous events to the developer through Umami (cloud.umami.is), a web analytics service that processes them on the developer’s behalf:

that the extension was installed or updated;
that a YouTube video page was opened (without the address, title or video identifier);
which type of action ran, for example “pause”, “next song”, “skip ad” or “search” (without what you said or searched for);
the speech language code in use (for example he-IL), the steps of a clarifying question (asked, yes, no), and a count of commands that were not understood;
the extension version and your browser’s interface language.
Never sent: your voice or audio, what you said, search phrases or song names, links or video identifiers, your account, name, email or any other personal information.

Like any web service, Umami’s servers receive technical request information such as an IP address and browser type. We use only aggregated statistics (for example counts by country, browser and language). Umami’s own privacy policy applies to how it handles this. The extension does not create user accounts, advertising identifiers or any persistent identifier of its own.

6. What we do not do
We do not sell user data and we do not use it for advertising or for credit or lending decisions.
We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension’s single purpose (voice control of YouTube).
We share data only with Umami as our analytics service provider, and where the law requires it.
The use of information received complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
7. Permissions
The extension needs access to YouTube pages only (youtube.com and its subdomains), to find the video player and control it (play, pause, seek, volume, speed, subtitles, full screen, skipping ads, moving between videos and running a search). It does not read other websites, and it does not request permissions for tabs, history, cookies or accounts. It does not download or run remote code.

8. Your choices
You can stop all reporting by uninstalling the extension, and you can turn off the microphone permission at any time.
Advanced users can stop the usage reports about commands by setting localStorage.ytVoiceAnalytics = '0' on youtube.com, and stop read-aloud questions with localStorage.ytVoiceSpeak = '0'.
We keep no information that identifies you. If you have a question about your data or want to ask us to delete anything related to you, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].
9. Changes to this policy
If the extension’s data practices change, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date before the new version is released.
